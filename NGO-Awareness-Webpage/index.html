About Us

InAmigos Foundation is an NGO working towards community upliftment through education, environment, animal welfare, and social development programs conducted by volunteers across India. Driven by a passionate network of young changemakers, the foundation turns collective empathy into measurable grassroots impact. By bridging gaps in society through collaborative action, InAmigos Foundation empowers local communities, fosters sustainable environmental practices, and champions the welfare of vulnerable animals, ultimately building a more compassionate and equitable society for all.
